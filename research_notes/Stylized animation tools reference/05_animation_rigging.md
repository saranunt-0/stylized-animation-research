## Q1. How did Arcane (Fortiche), Alberto Mielgo and Spider-Verse actually approach animation: timing, posing, keyframe vs mocap, software?

### Takeaway
All three are keyframe-first. Arcane was hand-keyframed in Maya with no mocap, using live-action reference. Mielgo's *The Witness* was "NO Mocap, NO rotoscope" keyframe. *Jibaro* was hand-keyed by Agora from multi-camera video of dancers, which was used as reference and not as mocap. Spider-Verse established "fluid" stepped timing, mixing ones and twos inside a single shot, plus multiples and smears instead of motion blur. Arcane's 12 fps hand-drawn FX against smoother character animation is widely reported, but I found it only in secondary sources.

**Spider-Verse (stepped-animation lineage)**

### Inferences
- All three reference productions reject raw mocap and use **live-action reference plus keyframing**. Mielgo's naturalism comes from closely studied reference, not from capture. For an indie project copying these looks, the most faithful pipeline is "shoot reference video, then keyframe". Mocap and AI video-mocap are best kept as blocking or reference aids (see Q4).
- "Stepped" should be treated as a per-shot, per-character and even per-moment decision (Beveridge's "fluid scale"), not a global render setting. Tooling needs per-object and per-range control (see Q2).
- Arcane's look comes more from painted textures, 2D FX on lower frame rates, and strong posing than from stepping the character animation itself. The S1/S2 character frame-rate policy is not confirmed by any primary source I could access.

### Gaps
- No primary Fortiche quote found stating the exact character frame rate policy (ones vs twos vs mixed). The 12 fps FX vs 24 fps characters split is reported only by aggregator sites.
- Fortiche rig specifics (proprietary Maya rigs, face system, 2D-face approaches) were not found in accessible sources.
- I could not open primary pages directly (see the sourcing note at the top). Quotes from befores & afters, AWN and SyncSketch are taken from search summaries and should be spot-checked before verbatim publication.
- Whether *Jibaro* or *The Windshield Wiper* used stepped timing is not documented in the sources found.

## Q2. Which free tools, add-ons and repos best support stepped and stylized animation in Blender (stepping, smears and multiples, posing to camera, squash & stretch, secondary motion)?

### Takeaway
The core free building block is Blender's own **Stepped Interpolation F-Curve modifier**: per F-curve, with step size, offset and frame-range limits, so it can vary per object and per shot. On top of that, the strongest free and open tools are **SMEAR** (GPL, SIGGRAPH 2024: elongated in-betweens, multiples and motion lines), Blender Studio's **Lattice Magic** (Camera Lattice for 2D-style cheats to camera), and the maintained **Wiggle Bones / Wiggle 2** forks for follow-through. AnimAide is no longer developed, and Animation Layers is paid.

### Cited Findings

**Stepping / on twos and threes**
- `[documentation]` **Stepped Interpolation F-Curve modifier** (built in, Blender 5.2 LTS manual): "gives the curve a stepped appearance by sampling it every N frames and making it hold its value after each sample". Options are Step Size, Offset (shifts which frames are held), Start Frame and End Frame (restrict the range). — [Blender 5.2 LTS Manual: F-Curve Modifiers](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/editors/graph_editor/fcurves/modifiers.html) (4.1 manual: [link](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.1/editors/graph_editor/fcurves/modifiers.html))
  - Historical bug in the restrict-frame-range IN/OUT parameters, now archived: [T52009](https://developer.blender.org/T52009) (an old tracker item; current status not verified)
- `[documentation]` The F-Curve **Constant** interpolation mode is the other built-in route (key every 2 frames with constant interpolation). — [Blender 5.2 LTS Manual: F-Curve Properties](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/editors/graph_editor/fcurves/properties.html); community how-tos: [Blender Artists: Animating on twos and threes](https://blenderartists.org/t/animating-on-twos-and-threes/1165704), [Blender Artists: Animation on twos](https://blenderartists.org/t/animation-on-twos/686600), [Blender Artists: How to get stepped animation to work](https://blenderartists.org/t/how-to-get-stepped-animation-to-work/599005)
- `[code-addon]` **Stop Motion Style Animator (Pro)** on the official Extensions platform. "Step Frames" holds poses on twos or threes without deleting keys, and "Phase Offset & Stagger" lets objects update on different frames (A on odd frames, B on even). Requires **Blender 5.0+**; found in the sidebar under StopMotion. Licence and price were not confirmed. — [extensions.blender.org: Stop Motion Animator](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/stop-motion-style-animator/)
- `[code-addon]` **ontwos-retimer** converts MMD physics into stepped on-twos/on-threes keyframes. Very new (created Sept 2026, 0 stars) and niche. — [GitHub: zlz188/ontwos-retimer](https://github.com/zlz188/ontwos-retimer)

**Smears, multiples, motion lines**
- `[code-addon]` **SMEAR** (Basset, Bénard, Barla; SIGGRAPH 2024 technical paper). A Blender add-on for elongated in-betweens, multiple in-betweens (transparent copies) and motion lines. It has a Python pre-process plus a Geometry Nodes modifier. v1.1.5, developed and tested on Blender **4.2 LTS**, and it works on rigged characters and simple meshes. Limitations: the "Camera POV" option only works on simple objects without skeletons, motion lines are randomly sampled, and multiples need a manual alpha material setup. The Extensions page lists **GPL-3.0-or-later**, while the paper itself is CC-BY 4.0. — [GitHub: MoStyle/SMEAR](https://github.com/MoStyle/SMEAR); [extensions.blender.org: SMEAR](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/smear/) ([version history](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/smear/versions/)); [ACM paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3641519.3657457); [project blog](https://mostyle.github.io/blog/sig2024/); [ACM SIGGRAPH blog: SMEAR Meets Sphere](https://blog.siggraph.org/2025/01/smear-meets-sphere.html/)
- `[documentation]` Follow-up research: "Trajectory-aware Smears for Stylized 3D Animations" (2025). — [ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3769047.3769057) (whether code is available is not checked)
- `[code-addon]` **Smudge Pencil** (Sander Joon) is a Geometry Nodes smear-frame setup for Blender **4.3+** that combines Blender Studio's Brushstroke Tools with a modified refraction shader. Gumroad, price not confirmed. — [Animation Hub](https://animationhub.eu/films/smudge-pencil-%E2%88%BC-create-smear-frames-%E2%88%BC-geometry-nodes-setup-for-blender-4-3/); [Gumroad](https://sanderjoon.gumroad.com/l/smudgepencil)
- `[code-addon]` **Smearify** makes screen-space smear frames by dragging lattice points (GN setup). Gumroad, price not confirmed. — [Gumroad: Smearify](https://andykxyz.gumroad.com/l/Smearify)
- `[tutorial]` General background on smears: [GarageFarm: Smear frames](https://garagefarm.net/blog/smear-frames-enhancing-motion-in-animation)

**Posing / cheating to camera**
- `[code-addon]` **Lattice Magic** (Blender Studio tool, on the Extensions platform). Its **Camera Lattice** creates a lattice in the camera's view frame and deforms a character or collection with it, a direct tool for 2D-style per-shot cheats. — [Blender Studio: Lattice Magic](https://studio.blender.org/tools/addons/lattice_magic)
- `[recreation]` Spider-Verse camera recreation in Blender. — [BlenderNation: Recreating Into the Spider-Verse's Camera](https://www.blendernation.com/2019/08/17/blender-breakdown-recreating-into-the-spiderverses-camera/)

**Secondary motion / squash-stretch helpers**
- `[code-addon]` **Wiggle 2** (shteeve3d, original). A ground-up rewrite of Wiggle Bones with bone jiggle physics, collisions, pinning and one-click bake to keys. **GPL-3.0**, about 1k stars, 66 open issues; supported Blender versions are not stated. — [GitHub: shteeve3d/blender-wiggle-2](https://github.com/shteeve3d/blender-wiggle-2)
- `[code-addon]` Maintained forks on Extensions: **Wiggle Bones**, a partial refactor "to ensure compatibility with Blender 5.0 and newer" (from jurassicjordan's fork of Labhatorian's fork), at [extensions.blender.org: Wiggle Bones](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/wiggle-bones/); **Wiggle 2: RTX Edition**, community-maintained, at [extensions.blender.org: Wiggle 2 RTX Edition](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/wiggle-2/); **EXea Jiggle** at [extensions.blender.org: EXea Jiggle](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/exea-jiggle/); comparison listing at [CG Dive: Wiggle bones](https://addons.cgdive.com/tools/wiggle-bones)
- `[code-addon]` **AnimAide** (CurveTools, AnimOffset, KeyManager: blend-to-neighbour, offset propagation, key types and interpolation). "Compatible with Blender 2.93 and up", but the author says **"Development no longer active"** and warns that Blender's new animation system "most likely will break AnimAide". Seeking a maintainer. Licence not confirmed (the LICENSE file URL returned 404). — [GitHub: aresdevo/animaide](https://github.com/aresdevo/animaide). **Flag: abandoned; test before relying on it in 4.4+/5.x.**
- `[documentation]` Blender 5.0 integrated **Copy Global Transform** into core (no longer an add-on; scripts that try to enable the add-on will break). It also added a "Geometry Attribute" constraint, a "Jump Time by Delta" operator and per-pose-bone visibility. — [Blender 5.0 Release Notes: Animation & Rigging](https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/5.0/animation_rigging/); 5.2 LTS notes at [developer.blender.org 5.2](https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/5.2/animation_rigging/); 5.0 overview at [blender.org 5.0](https://www.blender.org/releases/5-0/) and [CG Channel: Blender 5.0 key features](https://www.cgchannel.com/2025/11/blender-5-0-is-out-check-out-its-5-key-features/)

**Project Gold (Blender Studio's stylized 2D/3D film)**
- `[asset]`/`[talk]` Project Gold is Blender Studio's stylized-rendering showcase, with a dedicated painterly brush-stroke add-on built on Geometry Nodes, Grease Pencil and Cycles. Production pages include a "Brush Stroke: Stepping Artifact Fix" entry (breaking curves to accentuate plane breaks). — [Project Gold](https://studio.blender.org/projects/gold/); [Gold: Brush Strokes](https://studio.blender.org/projects/gold/3c7fb86d72b766/); [Gold Premiere blog](https://studio.blender.org/blog/project-gold-premiere/); [Storyboarding in Blender](https://studio.blender.org/blog/project-gold-storyboarding-in-blender/); [Production Log 04](https://studio.blender.org/blog/gold-production-log-04/); [Project Gold Update](https://studio.blender.org/blog/project-gold-update/)

### Inferences
- The minimum viable "Spider-Verse-style" kit in Blender 5.x is: the Stepped modifier per F-curve or object (use Offset to desynchronise characters and Start/End to switch between ones and twos inside a shot), SMEAR for multiples and smears, Lattice Magic Camera Lattice for cheats to camera, and a Wiggle fork for overlap that is baked before stepping.
- Because the Stepped modifier is non-destructive, animators can block on ones and preview on twos, which matches the "fluid scale" in Beveridge's quote.
- A driver/Geometry Nodes "frame quantise" trick (driving time with floor(frame/2)*2) is commonly used in the community for whole-object stepping but was not verified in this session. Treat it as UNVERIFIED.

### Gaps
- Licence and price of Stop Motion Style Animator Pro were not confirmed.
- No primary Blender Studio source confirms whether Project Gold's character animation was stepped. Its "stepping artifact" page concerns brush-stroke rendering, not animation timing.
- Motion paths tooling (built-in Motion Paths, third-party motion-trail add-ons) was not checked this session.
- I could not verify whether SMEAR has been updated for Blender 5.x (tested on 4.2 LTS).

## Q3. Which free rigs and rigging systems are production-proven for stylized films, and what free facial, 2D-face, pose-library and animation-layer tools exist?

### Takeaway
**CloudRig** (free, Blender Studio, on the Extensions platform) is the production-proven open rig generator. It built the rigs for Sprite Fright, Charge, Wing It! and **Gold**, and its rigs ship as CC-BY assets. Rigify remains the built-in baseline, and Auto-Rig Pro is the main paid alternative. For 2D faces, the free options are Grease Pencil face rigs and mesh-card or texture-swap tutorials, plus small GitHub tools such as Tiny 2D Rig Tools. Blender's Pose Library is built on the core asset system, and Animation Layers is paid.

### Cited Findings

**Rig generators / systems**
- `[code-addon]` **CloudRig** is Blender Studio's rig-generation and rigging-workflow extension "developed by the Blender Studio for our Open Movies". It works by setting parameters on a simple metarig, which then generates the control rig. Install via Blender's "Get Extensions" panel. — [CloudRig docs: Introduction](https://studio.blender.org/tools/addons/cloudrig/introduction); [Rig UI](https://studio.blender.org/tools/addons/cloudrig/rig-ui); [Workflow Enhancements](https://studio.blender.org/tools/addons/cloudrig/workflow-enhancements); [Contribute/code](https://studio.blender.org/tools/addons/cloudrig/code); source at [projects.blender.org/Mets/CloudRig](https://projects.blender.org/Mets/CloudRig); Extensions listing at extensions.blender.org/add-ons/cloudrig/ (URL as given in the Blender Studio text)

## Q4. What free and open-source mocap pipelines are practical for an indie stylized project in 2026, and what are their limitations? How do you clean mocap for stylized timing?

### Takeaway
The practical free options are: **FreeMoCap**, multi-webcam, AGPL-3.0, very active (v2.0.0-alpha.25 on 24 Sept 2026, with new Blender export options); **BlendCap**, single-video body, hands and face in Blender 4.2+ (GPL-3.0, needs an RTX-class GPU); the **BlendArMocap** MediaPipe family (original discontinued; Blender 4.2+ fork exists); and **Rokoko Video/Vision** (freemium, about 30 s/month free). Research models such as GVHMR are **non-commercial**. Given that Arcane and Mielgo used reference plus keyframe, mocap is best treated as blocking or reference that gets stepped and re-posed.

### Cited Findings
- `[code-addon]` **FreeMoCap** is "a free-and-open-source, hardware-and-software-agnostic, minimal-cost, research-grade, motion capture system". **AGPL-3.0** (maintainers open to alternative licensing), Python 3.9 to 3.11, NVIDIA GPU acceleration on Windows/Linux, CPU mode for macOS. About 10.4k stars. — [GitHub: freemocap/freemocap](https://github.com/freemocap/freemocap)
  - Releases: **v2.0.0-alpha.25 (Sept 24, 2026)**, alpha.24 (Sept 16), alpha.23 (Aug 27). v2 rebuilt the camera backend, tracking and UI, added Blender export options to the GUI, worked on hand detection, and fixed CPU-only runs. — [GitHub: freemocap releases](https://github.com/freemocap/freemocap/releases). Stable v1.8.2 (Apr 22, 2026) on [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/freemocap/) (search summary). Citation: [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/22131400)
  - `[code-addon]` **freemocap_blender_addon** loads FreeMoCap output into Blender with an auto-built skeleton and annotated video planes. AGPL-3.0. — [GitHub: freemocap_blender_addon](https://github.com/freemocap/freemocap_blender_addon) ([releases](https://github.com/freemocap/freemocap_blender_addon/releases), including a 2026.04 build per search summary); [freemocap_blendarmocap fork](https://github.com/freemocap/freemocap_blendarmocap)
- `[code-addon]` **BlendCap** captures single-video body, fingers and face in Blender. It uses Meta SAM 3D Body (127 joints) for body and hands, MediaPipe for face (drives ARKit-style shape keys), and YOLO11 for detection. Retargets to **Rigify, Auto-Rig Pro, CloudRig and Mixamo** (FK/IK) with built-in cleanup (foot grounding, depth-noise filtering, tracking-failure repair). **GPL-3.0-or-later** (includes an AGPL YOLO11 detector); "created animations are yours to use commercially". Blender **4.2+**, Windows/Linux, NVIDIA RTX 20 / GTX 16 or newer (6 GB+ VRAM), about 32 GB disk, about 11 GB one-time download. New (created Mar 2026). — [GitHub: Arcomade/BlendCap](https://github.com/Arcomade/BlendCap)
- `[code-addon]` **BlendArMocap** does MediaPipe pose, hand and face tracking in Blender with transfer to Rigify. GPL-3.0, about 1.1k stars. **"Discontinued"**: the author "cannot actively maintain" it. — [GitHub: cgtinker/BlendArMocap](https://github.com/cgtinker/BlendArMocap); docs at [cgtinker.github.io](https://cgtinker.github.io/BlendArMocap/installation/installation.html). Known MediaPipe dependency install issues: [issue #97](https://github.com/cgtinker/BlendArMocap/issues/97)
  - `[code-addon]` **NyuyenMocap-Oyen** fork: ported to the modern MediaPipe Tasks API, **Blender 4.2+**, GPLv3, adds a safe preview-armature adapter and a generic transfer config. Small (22 commits). — [GitHub: sadikinbancin/NyuyenMocap-Oyen](https://github.com/sadikinbancin/NyuyenMocap-Oyen)
- `[code-addon]` **Open Mocap** (MediaPipe, MIT): offline body and real-time hand tracking with retargeting, but **only Blender 4.0.0 and earlier**, so it is outdated for 5.x. — [GitHub: Larenju-Rai/open-mocap-blender](https://github.com/Larenju-Rai/open-mocap-blender)
- `[code-addon]` **GVHMR** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2024) gives world-grounded human motion from video. Its licence is a custom ZJU research licence: "educational, research and non-profit purposes only", and modifications are "prohibited for commercial use". — [GitHub: zju3dv/GVHMR](https://github.com/zju3dv/GVHMR) ([LICENSE](https://github.com/zju3dv/GVHMR/blob/main/LICENSE))
  - Wrappers and pipelines built on GVHMR: [mocap-wrapper (GVHMR, TRAM, WiLoR to Blender/Unreal)](https://github.com/AClon314/mocap-wrapper); [ComfyUI-Video2MotionCapture](https://github.com/AKASubaz/ComfyUI-Video2MotionCapture); [mixamo-llm-mocap (GVHMR to Mixamo rig in Blender via MCP, created Aug 2026)](https://github.com/squall01337/mixamo-llm-mocap). These inherit GVHMR's non-commercial restriction.
- `[code-addon]` **WHAM** (CVPR 2024) is a world-grounded video-to-SMPL model. The code is **MIT**, but users must register separately to download SMPL body models. — [GitHub: yohanshin/WHAM](https://github.com/yohanshin/WHAM)
- `[code-addon]` Other repos: [pose2sim (multi-camera markerless to OpenSim, with Blender visualisation)](https://github.com/perfanalytics/pose2sim); [b3d_mocap_import (EasyMocap, FrankMocap, VIBE output into Blender)](https://github.com/carlosedubarreto/b3d_mocap_import) and [ceb_hubmocap (modular mocap hub add-on)](https://github.com/carlosedubarreto/ceb_hubmocap); [SnowMocap (camera-array mocap)](https://github.com/liaochikon/SnowMocap); [KinectMocap4Blender](https://github.com/moraell/KinectMocap4Blender); [QuickMocap (import/clean .npz/.pkl from ROMP etc.)](https://github.com/vltmedia/QuickMocap-BlenderAddon)
- `[code-addon]` Retargeting: **Expy Kit** converts and renames between rig types (Rigify, Mixamo, UE) with action-range and bake utilities. 438 stars; licence not stated on the page. — [GitHub: pKrime/Expy-Kit](https://github.com/pKrime/Expy-Kit). **Rokoko Studio Live for Blender** does live streaming and retargeting (body, 52 face blendshapes, fingers, up to 5 actors), **LGPL-3.0**, Blender 2.80+. — [GitHub: Rokoko/rokoko-studio-live-blender](https://github.com/Rokoko/rokoko-studio-live-blender)
- `[code-addon]` (freemium) **Rokoko Vision / Rokoko Video** AI video mocap. Free Starter tier gives **30 s of Vision AI per month**, unlimited text-to-motion and FBX export. Basic is about **$10/mo billed annually** (600 s/month, custom retargeting, BVH). Plus is about **$20/mo billed annually**. — [Rokoko pricing](https://www.rokoko.com/pricing); [Rokoko Vision 3.0](https://www.rokoko.com/products/vision) (search summary; verify on the vendor page)
- Cleanup aids: BlendCap's built-in foot grounding and filtering (above), Animation Layers for layered mocap offsets ([Superhive](https://superhivemarket.com/products/animation-layers)), and Cascadeur's "animation unbaking for mocap" (see Q5; [Cascadeur plans](https://cascadeur.com/plans)).

### Inferences
- **Recommended indie path in 2026:** shoot multi-angle reference as Mielgo did for Jibaro. Optionally run it through FreeMoCap (multi-cam, AGPL tool; the output data is yours) or BlendCap (single cam, GPL), retarget to a CloudRig or Rigify character, then *re-key it*. Extract key poses, delete in-betweens, push poses to camera (Lattice Magic), and apply the Stepped modifier with varied step and offset. This turns mocap into reference and blocking rather than final motion.
- Avoid GVHMR-based pipelines for commercial work unless licensed. Check the SMPL/SMPL-X model licences before using WHAM output commercially.
- MediaPipe-only Blender add-ons are fragile across Blender and Python upgrades: the original is discontinued and Open Mocap is capped at 4.0.

### Gaps
- SMPL/SMPL-X licence terms for commercial use were not checked in this session.
- Move.ai, DeepMotion, QuickMagic and Plask free tiers were not checked (search budget exhausted).
- No source was found that documents a published "mocap to stepped stylized" cleanup workflow from a studio. The workflow above is inference.
- Accuracy and limits of FreeMoCap v2 alpha (hands, face, occlusion) are not documented on the pages read.
